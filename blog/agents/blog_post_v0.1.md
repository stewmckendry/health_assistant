### From Dr. AI to the Digital Chief

Inspired by Microsoft’s “Medical Diagnosis Orchestrator,” we asked:  
*What would certified AI agents for Ontario healthcare look like?*

The answer: **The Ontario Healthcare AI Agents Registry**  
A demonstration of how AI specialists can collaborate, share authoritative knowledge, and relieve clinicians of administrative burden.

---

## Meet the Team

### 🧾 Dr. OFF (Ontario Finance & Formulary)
- Billing and benefits expert
- Knows 8,401 ODB-listed drugs, 4,166 OHIP billing codes, and 11 categories of assistive devices
- Answers questions like:  
    *“Can I bill C124 for this patient?”*  
    *“Is Ozempic covered for weight loss?”*

### ⚖️ Dr. OPA (Ontario Practice Advisor)
- Digital advisor versed in CPSO policies, Ontario Health pathways, PHO infection control, and CEP clinical tools
- Explains what physicians can and cannot do
- Provides up-to-date guidance from provincial sources

### 📚 Agent 97
- Evolution of My Health Assistant
- Draws from 97 trusted medical sources (NEJM to Ontario Health)
- Speaks plain language, redirects emergencies, and provides numbered citations

### 🧩 The Chief
- The orchestrator
- Routes complex questions, synthesizes expert opinions, and resolves conflicts between agents
- Delivers answers with citations and confidence scores

---

## How It Works

These agents don’t just search the web.  
They combine:
- Structured data (databases of codes and formularies)
- Embedded Ontario-specific knowledge
- Selective live searches restricted to official domains

**What sets the registry apart:**  
Built for accuracy and accountability—not marketing spin or guesswork.

---

## A Glimpse of the Future

This model hints at a future where Ontario certifies clinical AI agents—each evaluated, versioned, and monitored like medical devices.

**The long game:**
- Hospitals hosting their own “Agent Registries”
- Provincial certification for safe deployment
- APIs replacing PDFs, enabling machines to read, reason, and cross-check instantly

---

## The “So What”

This registry demonstrates something subtle but profound:

> **AI isn’t here to replace judgment. It’s here to route, reconcile, and retrieve—so clinicians can focus on care.**

The next time someone says “AI will replace doctors,” show them The Chief.  
It’s not a replacement—it’s a rehearsal for the future.
