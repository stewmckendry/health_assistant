💬 **Coaching the Machine, Part 2 — Building Ontario's Clinical AI Agent Registry**

Together with Will Falk and Dr. Keith Thompson, we've built a working demonstration of what certified clinical AI agents could look like for Ontario.

Inspired by Microsoft's "Medical AI Diagnostic Orchestrator" and Google's Health AI Agent concept, we created four specialized agents that collaborate like a real clinical team:

🧩 **Chief Resident** – Orchestrates multi-agent consultation for complex diagnostic and treatment planning questions, synthesizing evidence, regulatory guidance, and coverage information

🧾 **Dr. OFF** – Ontario Finance & Formulary specialist providing OHIP billing codes, ODB drug coverage, and ADP device funding guidance

⚖️ **Dr. OPA** – Ontario Practice Advisor delivering real-time guidance on CPSO policies, quality standards, clinical pathways, and infection control

📚 **Agent 97** – Evidence-based clinical search across 97 trusted medical sources including NEJM, Lancet, JAMA, PubMed, Cochrane, UpToDate, and authoritative clinical guidelines

Each agent is grounded in authoritative Ontario and medical data: 8,401 ODB drugs, 4,166 OHIP billing codes, CPSO policies, Public Health Ontario IPAC guidelines, Centre for Effective Practice clinical tools, Ontario Health quality standards, Choosing Wisely recommendations, and leading international medical journals and guidelines.

This is a **demonstration, not a product.** But it points toward a future where healthcare organizations maintain **registries of certified AI agents**—each trained on vetted data, evaluated for accuracy and safety, versioned for auditability, and approved for clinical use.
